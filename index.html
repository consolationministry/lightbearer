WHO WE ARE

Carrying the light
of Christ, together.

C&S Bible Church is a worshipping community rooted in Scripture, prayer, and the transforming presence of God.

We are committed to bringing the Cherubim and Seraphim Church back into the full light of God's sight: strengthening our devotion to the Bible, restoring a living culture of prayer, and making worship a witness that draws every generation closer to Jesus. From darkness to light, from hearing the Word to living it, we walk this journey together.

OUR MISSION

To lead people from darkness to the light of Christ through the Bible, prayer, worship, and a life of loving service.

OUR VISION

A renewed C&S church, faithful to Scripture and alive in the Spirit, shining Christ's hope in every community.
